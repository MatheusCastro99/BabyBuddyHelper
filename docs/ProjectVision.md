# Project Vision

## Summary

BabyBuddyHelper is an offline-first parenting companion
that helps caregivers organize routines, appointments,
and baby-care activities in a warm and encouraging environment.

---

BabyBuddyHelper is evolving from a simple task and appointment manager into an:

Offline-first parenting companion that helps caregivers organize routines, appointments, and baby-care activities in a warm, 
encouraging, and supportive environment.

The long-term goal is NOT to become another productivity application.

The desired user experience is:

- Warm
- Friendly
- Supportive
- Low stress
- Family-oriented
- Encouraging

Future UX direction includes a lightweight companion mascot that provides a welcoming experience and surfaces helpful information.

Examples:

- Today's appointments
- Outstanding tasks
- Positive reinforcement
- Gentle reminders

The companion is NOT intended to be a general-purpose AI chatbot.

Think:

- Duolingo Owl
- Microsoft Clippy (without annoyance)
- Calm app mascots

rather than ChatGPT.

A later phase may give the companion a carefully scoped AI helper for tips and basic questions, within clear guidelines. It is not a doctor or therapist and never replaces professional advice.
